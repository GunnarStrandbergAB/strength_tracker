Coaching Insights — the app now coaches you.

This is the biggest update since launch. HellBentIron now has a coaching layer that gives you contextual feedback before, during, and after every workout. It gets smarter the more you train.

WHAT'S NEW

Post-Workout Debrief
After every workout you'll see a summary sheet with your stats, a quality score ring, and 2-3 coaching bullets pulled from your actual data — PRs, load warnings, overload trends, volume changes, recovery notes, and comparisons to your most similar past session. Not filler text. Real insights.

Pre-Workout Context
Before you start, a new card shows recovery status per muscle group (green/orange/red), your ACWR training load gauge, and adherence stats (days since last workout, weekly frequency, streak). Available at 5+ workouts.

Weight Suggestions
Each set now shows a suggested weight based on your recent e1RM, adjusted for recovery and training load. Rounded to the nearest plate. Shows below the "PREVIOUS" line.

Effort Creep Warnings
If your RPE is climbing session over session without strength gains, the app flags it on the exercise card. Catches the problem before it becomes a stall.

Exercise Hints
Inline one-liners per exercise: plateau warnings, absence reminders, trend updates, recovery notes. Shows in the exercise card header.

Weekly Digest
A dashboard card (Mon-Wed) comparing this week vs last: workout count, volume delta, PRs, and a top coaching insight.

Training Archetypes
Your workouts are clustered into patterns (Push Day, Leg Day, Full Body, etc.) using k-means on the workout vectors. A training fingerprint tracks how varied and stable your routine is.

Trajectory Analysis
Tracks the velocity and acceleration of your training changes through vector space. Detects plateaus, deceleration, and "spinning wheels" (changing a lot but going nowhere).

Achievements
6 badges: Progressive Loader, Plateau Breaker, Iron Consistency, Balanced Builder, Volume Explorer, Smart Recovery.

Change Point Detection
Detects significant shifts in your training pattern and tells you what changed.

Time-of-Day Analysis (20+ workouts)
Compares quality scores across morning/afternoon/evening/night to find your best training window.

Volume-Response Curves (50+ workouts)
Personal MEV/MAV/MRV derived from your own data via quadratic regression.

KNOWN ISSUES
- Weekly digest card only shows Mon-Wed and requires a non-empty previous week
- Quality score donut animation may stutter on older devices
- Volume-response curves need 50+ workouts so most testers won't see them yet

All coaching runs on-device. No data sent anywhere.
