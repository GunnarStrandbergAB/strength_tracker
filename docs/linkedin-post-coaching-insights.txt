Most fitness apps are either dumb loggers or cloud-dependent AI.

We just shipped a coaching insights system for HellBentIron that does something different: research-grade training intelligence running entirely on your iPhone. No cloud. No subscriptions. No data collection. The same math that sports scientists use in lab settings, running directly on-device.

Here's what makes it different from everything else out there.


Every workout is an 18-dimensional vector.

When you finish a session, the app encodes it as a point in mathematical space — volume, intensity, muscle targeting, compound ratio, rest patterns, RPE, time of day. Cosine similarity across these 18 dimensions finds patterns no human would notice. Your "Push Day" from last Tuesday was 87% similar to the one three weeks ago — but you moved 12% more volume. That comparison happens in milliseconds using Apple's Accelerate framework, not a server.


Coaching that speaks when it matters.

Most apps bury insights in a dashboard you open once and forget. We built three coaching surfaces that meet you where you already are:

After every workout, a debrief sheet shows your stats, a quality score donut, and 2-3 contextual coaching bullets. These aren't generic motivational quotes — they're priority-ranked from 8 data sources: PRs, ACWR load warnings, quality deltas, overload trends, volume changes, recovery notes, and comparisons against your most similar past session.

Before you start, a context card shows recovery status per muscle group, your Acute:Chronic Workload Ratio gauge, and adherence stats (days since last workout, current streak, dropout risk).

During logging, each exercise gets inline coaching: a weight suggestion based on e1RM extrapolation with Brzycki inverse, adjusted for recovery (-10% if fatigued, -15% if ACWR is in the danger zone), rounded to the nearest 2.5 kg plate. If your RPE is climbing without strength gains, an effort creep warning tells you before it becomes a problem.


Intelligence that grows with you.

The system starts simple — just stats after your first workout. By workout 5, you get quality scores and weight suggestions. At 10, plateau detection and achievement badges. By 19, it's tracking your training trajectory through vector space, clustering your workout patterns via k-means++, and predicting plateaus weeks before they happen. At 50 workouts, it derives your personal volume-response curves — your own MEV, MAV, and MRV from quadratic regression on your actual training data.

24 features unlock progressively. Nothing shows up until there's enough data for it to be meaningful.


Deterministic by design.

Same inputs, same outputs. Every time. No random AI hallucinations, no "the model was having an off day." The e1RM calculations, CUSUM change point detection, silhouette-scored clustering, and EWMA-smoothed quality trends all produce identical results given identical data.

On iOS 26+, Apple Intelligence rewrites coaching text through on-device Foundation Models for more natural phrasing — but the underlying analysis is always deterministic math.


Your data never leaves your device.

No accounts. No cloud sync. No analytics SDK. No telemetry. Zero data collection. The entire system — 7 coaching services, 29 analytics services, 12 new domain models — runs in your app sandbox. If you want to share data, there's a webhook you explicitly configure. Otherwise, your training history is yours alone.
