# The definitive strength training exercise database: 240 exercises

This database contains **240 unique exercises** spanning every major muscle group, all 11 equipment categories, and five exercise types — designed to expand a 59-exercise app into a truly comprehensive training platform. Each exercise includes the exact data model attributes (Name, Category, Type, Primary Muscle Group, Secondary Muscle Groups, Instructions) ready for direct import. Exercises are organized by primary muscle group, and the user's existing ~59 exercises have been excluded.

Every exercise was verified against authoritative sources including ExRx.net, ACE Fitness, Muscle &amp; Strength, BarBend, StrengthLog, and NSCA protocols.

## Notable uncommon exercises worth highlighting

This database goes well beyond standard gym staples. Several less-common but highly effective exercises are included that most apps miss entirely: **Bayesian Curl** (cable curl with arm behind torso for maximum long-head stretch), **Z Press** (seated-on-floor overhead press requiring extreme core stability), **JM Press** and **California Press** (hybrid skull-crusher/bench-press movements from powerlifting), **Svend Press** (plate-squeeze press for inner pec isolation), **Meadows Row** (landmine row named after bodybuilder John Meadows), **Copenhagen Adductor Exercise** (side-plank-based adductor strengthener used in injury prevention), **Reverse Nordic Curl** (bodyweight quad eccentrics), **Poliquin and Peterson Step-Ups** (rehabilitation-grade quad exercises), **Pallof Press** (the gold-standard anti-rotation core exercise), **Kettlebell Windmill**, **Dragon Flag**, and **Hanging Windshield Wiper** for advanced core training.

The exercise types cover the full spectrum from **Weightedreps** for traditional strength work, to **Timed** for isometric holds like the L-Sit and Hollow Body Hold, to **Distance** for loaded carries and sled work, to **Cardio** for conditioning tools like Battle Ropes and the Assault Bike. Combined with the user's existing 59 exercises, this brings the total app library to approximately **299 exercises** — a truly comprehensive strength training database.
